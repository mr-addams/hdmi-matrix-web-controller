# Code of Conduct

This project follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) version 2.1.

## Our standard

We want this to be a welcoming place for everyone. Be respectful: disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and credit others' work.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer through a GitHub issue marked as a conduct report or privately through the contact in the repository profile. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may restrict participants who repeatedly violate them.
